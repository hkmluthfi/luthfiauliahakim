About

— a little about me —

I am an observer by nature, always paying close attention to people, environments, and data. As a fresh graduate in Geodesy and Geomatics Engineering from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), with an academic exchange experience at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM), I enjoy transforming real-world environments into meaningful spatial insights. My interests span GIS, surveying, spatial analysis, and project management, while I continue to strengthen my skills in data analysis and data-driven decision-making. Through my internship at PT Anugerah Samudera Madanindo, I gained hands-on experience supporting survey operations for the Patimban Port development, reinforcing my appreciation for precision, collaboration, and delivering reliable solutions in complex projects.
