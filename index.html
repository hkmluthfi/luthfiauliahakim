About the Author

A geodesy and geomatics engineer shaped by the rigour of Institut Teknologi Bandung — trained to read the earth not merely as terrain, but as a system of relationships: spatial, temporal, and analytical. The work lives at the intersection of classical surveying discipline and contemporary geospatial technology.

The interest is not in maps as objects, but in what they reveal: patterns of risk, infrastructure vulnerability, resource distribution, and the quiet geometry underlying every human settlement. GIS is the language; spatial analysis is the method; precision is the obligation.

Open to opportunities in GIS analysis, geospatial engineering, remote sensing, disaster risk assessment, infrastructure mapping, and the energy sector — wherever spatial thinking is an asset.
